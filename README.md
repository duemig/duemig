# David Dümig

Quantitative researcher in systematic equities, based in Berlin. I work on signal research, return forecasting and portfolio construction, with a focus on the cross-section of stock returns.

Director, Head of Equity Selection at Ultramarin. MSc Finance, Frankfurt School of Finance & Management.

## Selected work

| Repository | What it is |
|---|---|
| [ml-cross-sectional-stock-selection](https://github.com/duemig/ml-cross-sectional-stock-selection) | Master's thesis: penalised linear models, tree ensembles and neural networks forecasting monthly returns of about 4,000 developed-market stocks, 1989 to 2018 |
| [consumer-spend-signal](https://github.com/duemig/consumer-spend-signal) | Credit card spending as an equity signal: a point-in-time research pipeline and simulation study, with tests and CI |
| [Stanford LSTM project](https://github.com/duemig/Stanford-Project-Predicting-stock-prices-using-a-LSTM-Network) | CS230 project: LSTM networks against regularised logistic regression for the monthly direction of the S&P 500 |
| [Global financial crisis risk](https://github.com/duemig/LSE-Project-Analysing-the-Global-Financial-Crisis) | Value at risk, expected shortfall and copulas for ten S&P 500 stocks through the 2007-09 crisis |
| [normal-random-number-generation](https://github.com/duemig/normal-random-number-generation) | Inverse transform, rejection sampling and Box-Muller, checked with normality tests |
| [next-day-market-direction-case-study](https://github.com/duemig/next-day-market-direction-case-study) | 2020 interview case study: logistic regression and random forests for next-day market direction |

Most of my professional work is proprietary and not public.
