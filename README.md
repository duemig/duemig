# David Dümig

Quantitative researcher in systematic equities, based in Berlin. I work on signal research, return forecasting and portfolio construction, with a focus on the cross-section of stock returns.

Director, Head of Equity Selection at Ultramarin. MSc Finance, Frankfurt School of Finance & Management.

## Selected work

| Repository | What it is | Origin |
|---|---|---|
| [ml-cross-sectional-stock-selection](https://github.com/duemig/ml-cross-sectional-stock-selection) | Penalised linear models, tree ensembles and neural networks forecasting monthly returns of about 4,000 developed-market stocks, 1989 to 2018 | Master's thesis, Frankfurt School |
| [consumer-spend-signal](https://github.com/duemig/consumer-spend-signal) | Credit card spending as an equity signal: a point-in-time research pipeline and simulation study, with tests and CI | Take-home assignment for Man Group, presented at the interview; rebuilt on simulated data |
| [Stanford LSTM project](https://github.com/duemig/Stanford-Project-Predicting-stock-prices-using-a-LSTM-Network) | LSTM networks against regularised logistic regression for the monthly direction of the S&P 500 | Coursework, CS230 Deep Learning, Stanford |
| [LSE global financial crisis risk](https://github.com/duemig/LSE-Project-Analysing-the-Global-Financial-Crisis) | Value at risk, expected shortfall and copulas for ten S&P 500 stocks through the 2007-09 crisis | Coursework, risk management, LSE |
| [normal-random-number-generation](https://github.com/duemig/normal-random-number-generation) | Four ways to generate normal random numbers from a uniform generator, tested and used in a Monte Carlo VaR | Take-home project for a PhD interview at UCL, presented at the interview |
| [next-day-market-direction-case-study](https://github.com/duemig/next-day-market-direction-case-study) | Logistic regression and random forests for next-day market direction from 27 anonymised indicators | Take-home case study for Union Investment, presented at the interview |
